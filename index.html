CBD (cannabidiol) is a naturally occurring compound found in the hemp plant. It works by interacting with your body's endocannabinoid system (ECS), a complex cell-signaling system that helps regulate functions like sleep, mood, appetite, pain response, and immune function. Unlike THC, CBD is non-psychoactive and won't get you "high." Our top-rated CBD oils, like Arkos Bioscience's nano-formulated tincture, are designed to maximize this interaction through enhanced bioavailability.

Full Spectrum CBD contains all cannabinoids, terpenes, and flavonoids from the hemp plant, including trace amounts of THC (under 0.3%). This provides the "entourage effect" where compounds work together synergistically. Broad Spectrum is similar but has THC completely removed. CBD Isolate is pure CBD with all other compounds removed. In our testing, we found that proprietary nano CBD (like Arkos Bioscience) offers superior absorption regardless of spectrum type, making it the most effective option overall.
